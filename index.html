Help

Floor plan mode: press Floor plan (PDF), then Load PDF or image. Next press Set scale, tap two points on the plan with a known distance (a wall, a door), and type the real length. After that the Ruler, cameras, UniFi APs, walls and cubicles use true size. Map and Floor plan are saved separately, and you can switch between them any time.

The plan saves itself in this browser. Save as PDF fits the current map view on one landscape page, with extra sharp satellite tiles (it can take a few seconds to load them): frame the map first (Fit all helps), press the button, and in the print window choose Save as PDF as the printer. Use Save to file to keep a copy or move it to another computer, and Open file to load it. First place the Office, then poles. Plain poles with no cable of their own get a dashed Cat6 line to the nearest powered pole or the office. To draw a cable yourself, pick Cat6 or Fiber, then tap the first pole and the second pole. Cat6 over 300 ft turns red. Lengths are straight lines on the satellite map, so leave 10 to 15 percent extra for real runs. The map needs internet. Camera: pick Camera and tap a pole, or tap anywhere on the map, to put a camera there. The blue beam is its view and the camera sits at its base (a 360 camera sits in the middle of the circle). Tap a camera to select it. A white dot then appears at the end of the beam. Drag the dot to turn the camera, and drag it farther or closer to change the range (default 107 degrees and about 115 ft). Change the angle with the list at the bottom. To move a free camera, just drag its icon. You can put several cameras on one pole. Fence: pick Fence, tap each corner on the map, then tap Finish fence. Barrier gate: pick Barrier gate and tap the map where the base stands, then drag the white dot to turn the arm and set its length. The length is shown on the arm. Hand mode only moves and zooms the map. In every other mode except the cable tools you can drag poles, the office and cameras to move them.

Tip: hold Space while the mouse is over the map or plan and drag to move it (like the Hand tool). Without a mouse, drag empty space to move the map.
